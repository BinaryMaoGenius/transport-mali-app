## 🚀 Overview
The **Transport Mali App** is a digital solution aimed at modernizing urban and inter-city transportation logistics in Mali. It connects passengers, drivers, and transport agencies into a single, cohesive digital ecosystem to improve efficiency and reliability.

## ✨ Key Features
- 📍 **Route Tracking**: Live monitoring of transport routes and schedules.
- 🎟️ **Digital Booking**: Seamless reservation and ticketing system.
- 👨‍✈️ **Driver Portal**: Dedicated interface for drivers to manage trips.
- 📈 **Agency Analytics**: Tools for transport companies to track performance and optimize routes.

## 🎯 Impact
Designed to solve real-world logistical challenges in the Malian transport sector, promoting safety, predictability, and digital integration.
